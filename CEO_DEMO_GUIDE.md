# CEO Demo Guide: three minutes

Start with `START.bat`. Set the reporting month to **Nov 2026** and leave all filters cleared before you begin.

## Script

1. **Executive Overview (about 40 seconds).** Point to 44 targeted firms, 4,931 portfolio companies in the November snapshot, 3,403 with detailed profiles, and 24,493 unique news items published April to October. Open section A of the executive summary. Say that the November workbook holds October news and April news sits in the May workbook.

2. **Filter by firm (20 seconds).** Choose **Accel-KKR** in the PE firm dropdown. The overview narrows to one firm with 94 portfolio companies. Click **Reset filters**.

3. **PE account profile (25 seconds).** Open **PE Account Intelligence** and click **Andreessen Horowitz**, the largest account in the view. Show the snapshot history and the industry and geography exposure. Note that historical associations are snapshot records, not verified ownership.

4. **Portfolio company (25 seconds).** Open **Portfolio Intelligence**. Click **1stdibs** (Index Ventures). It shows reported revenue of 89.6 and IT spend of 29.9 USD million, with two captured signals and a High-priority hypothesis. Point out that companies without reported financials show "Not reported", not zero.

5. **Market signal and source (25 seconds).** Open **Market Signals**. Filter the level to Portfolio company. Click any High-relevance item, then open the original source link. Say that a news item is not a confirmed transaction.

6. **Industry drill-down (20 seconds).** Open **Industry Intelligence**. Click **Enterprise Software** (1,513 companies in the view) to see its companies in a drawer.

7. **Priority opportunity (25 seconds).** Open **Opportunity Intelligence**. The top Critical hypotheses score 97.5 and tie to CapitalG and its portfolio companies. Open one. Walk through the nine questions, the score components and the next step. Say clearly that this is a hypothesis for validation.

8. **Stakeholders (15 seconds).** Open **Stakeholder Intelligence**. Show that 201 contacts are Confirmed as of 4 September and that 27 need review. Open **Tyler Buckingham** (Accel-KKR, VP, High confidence). Note that successor candidates are unconfirmed and that emails are not shown.

9. **Outreach (15 seconds).** Open **Outreach Intelligence**. Show that 194 stakeholders have an outreach date. Say that these are recorded fields to confirm with the relationship owners, not evidence of engagement.

10. **Close (5 seconds).** Return to the overview and state the two caveats: the financial coverage is thin, and only 15 of 44 targeted firms have stakeholder records.

## If asked

- **Why do some numbers differ from the earlier summary?** The source summary reports 43 firms and 5,622 companies. The data gives 44 and 5,623. The difference is being confirmed with the data owner.
- **Can the scores be used as win probabilities?** No. They rank how strongly the evidence points to a capability fit.
- **Does the platform use AI?** No. The narrative is built from computed figures. No external AI or internet service is called.
- **Does it store contact emails?** No.
- **Can it run on the network?** It binds to 127.0.0.1 only and is not published.
